Beginner's guide

Your first Stremio or Nuvio setup, step by stepGuide by supermaniac75

Install Stremio or Nuvio, create your account, decide whether you'll use Debrid and let the wizard set everything up for you. Follow the steps in order: no technical knowledge needed, and every unusual word is explained below.

Words you'll see

No need to memorize them. Come back here if one confuses you.

  • Addon: a piece you add to Stremio or Nuvio to show catalogs, links or subtitles. The apps come almost empty: addons are what bring the content.
  • AIOStreams: an addon that gathers links from many sources into one list and sorts them by quality and language. It's the heart of the setup.
  • AIOMetadata: the addon that shows title pages, posters, descriptions and catalogs in your language.
  • Debrid: a paid service (TorBox, Real-Debrid and others) that downloads the video for you and delivers it fast and without buffering. It's optional.
  • API key: a long code your Debrid service gives you so the wizard can connect to it. It's private, like a password.
  • Backup: a copy of your current addon list that the wizard makes before changing anything, so you can go back.
  • P2P: downloading directly from other users. It works without Debrid, but it can be slower and those users can see your IP address.

1Install Stremio or Nuvio

Pick one app to start with. They use separate accounts and store their addons separately. You can set up the second app later.

Stremio

Open the official downloads page ↗ and choose your system: Windows, Mac, Android, TV or the option for your device. Install and open the app.

Nuvio

Go to nuvio.tv ↗ and pick the download for your phone, computer or TV. Download the app from its official site.

If you want to use the app on a TV, create the account and complete the wizard from a computer or phone. Then sign in on the TV with the same account. On iPhone, check what each app's official page offers, since it can vary by region.

2Create your account in the app

  1. In Stremio or Nuvio, tap Sign up or Create account and use an email address you can access.
  2. Confirm your email if the app asks you to. Keep the email and password: you'll use them in the wizard.
  3. Open the app and sign in once to check the account works. If Nuvio asks for a profile, create or choose the one you'll use.
Each app needs its own account. If you have both Stremio and Nuvio, create or use an account in each. The wizard can copy addons between them after you set up the first one.

3Decide whether you'll use Debrid

A Debrid service is optional. The wizard can use it to get playback links. You can also start without one. Link availability, quality and speed can vary.

I want to use TorBox

For its price and quality, I recommend the TorBox Essential plan to start. Sign up with your email and a TorBox password of its own (different from your Stremio or Nuvio one) and verify your email. Check the current price and terms before paying, and choose Essential. Then copy your API key from Settings → Account → API Key. The wizard will ask for it in step 3.

I don't want Debrid yet

In step 3 of the wizard, tap “I don't have Debrid”. The wizard will use free sources. Some download the video from other users (P2P): they can take longer to start and those users can see your IP address. You can add Debrid later by running the wizard again.

4Set up with the wizard

Open the Stremio & Nuvio Wizard in another tab. It has five steps and nothing changes in your account until you press the final button.

  1. Account: choose Stremio or Nuvio, type the email and password of that app and tap “Connect account”. In Nuvio, also choose the profile you want to set up. In Stremio, “Remember account in this browser” is optional: it keeps your encrypted sign-in only in that browser.
  2. Backup: tap “Create secure backup”. The wizard saves your current addon list (and, in Nuvio, the home rows) so you can go back. The backup stays encrypted in your browser for 30 days. You don't need this step's “Advanced options”.
  3. Preferences: at the top, pick a starting point: Recommended, Minimal, Full or HTTP only (no torrents, no Debrid). Then choose your main language (English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, German or Dutch). Under “Audio and subtitle languages (advanced)” you can add other languages and order them, for example English first and Japanese next for anime. If you have Debrid, keep “I have Debrid” and paste your API key; otherwise tap “I don't have Debrid”. In Nuvio, keep “Nuvio home collections” checked. Under “Additional content preferences” you can turn on Kids profile, anime, Remove 4K or “Cached only”, which shows only videos your Debrid already has ready, so they start instantly.
  4. Addons: the recommended ones are already checked. TMDB and TVDB work with the built-in key: you don't need accounts there. The MDBList field is optional (see “Track what you watch”). Under “Posters with ratings” you can pick PostersPlus, free and keyless, to show rating, genre and awards on every poster, or paste your own service's pattern. Under “Home catalogs” you choose which groups of rows to show, and a free Gemini key turns on AI search. The “Spanish-language addons” section is only useful if you watch content in Spanish. Under “Personalized recommendations” you can add Watchly, which adds “Because you watched…” rows.
  5. Review and apply: tap “Refresh plan”, read the summary of what will be added or replaced, and tap “Apply to Stremio” or “Apply to Nuvio”.

On the final screen you can also customize your home: move addons up or down and, in Stremio, hide or rename catalogs. The wizard makes another backup before saving. You'll also see how to connect Trakt if you use it.

When you finish, save your AIOStreams details. The final screen shows an AIOStreams user (UUID) and password. Tap “Save in browser” or “Download for password manager”. The wizard doesn't keep them, and you'll need them if you ever want to change that setup.

5Recommended addons

A short list is easier to maintain. The wizard gathers the main sources in AIOStreams and always installs AIOMetadata for title pages and catalogs.

Checked by default

  • AIOStreamsGathers and sorts links from many sources.
  • AIOMetadataTitle pages, posters and catalogs in your language. Always installed.
  • SubSenseSearches subtitles across several sources.
  • OpenSubtitles PROA second subtitle provider, in case the first finds nothing.
  • StreailerTrailers and season recaps.
  • Debrid storeShows what you already have saved in your Debrid. Only if you use Debrid.

Spanish-language addons (optional)

  • PeerflixMany Spanish-language links, configured automatically.
  • CometaA source focused on Spanish-language content; works with or without Debrid.
  • LatinobridLatin American Spanish only. Requires TorBox or Premiumize.
  • LinkañolSpain Spanish, useful for older titles. With TorBox or AllDebrid.
  • AnimESAnime in Spanish.

Recommendations (optional)

  • WatchlyRecommendation rows based on what you watch. It gets read access to your history, never your password.

Torrentio, Comet, MediaFusion and other well-known sources are already inside AIOStreams: no need to install them separately. If an addon doesn't respond on the day you use the wizard, it's skipped and the rest still installs; the final screen tells you which one was left out.

Only use content you have the right to access. Third-party addons are independent of Stremio, Nuvio and this wizard, and they may change or stop working.

6Check that it works

  1. Open Stremio or Nuvio with the same account you set up. If it was already open, close and reopen it. In Nuvio, enter the same profile.
  2. Search for a well-known movie and open its page. AIOStreams links should appear in the sources list. In Nuvio you'll also see new rows on the home screen: Watch Now, Genres, Decades and more.
  3. If nothing shows up, wait a minute and reopen the app.
  4. If you didn't like something, go back to step 2 of the wizard, open “Restore a previous setup” and choose the backup that was created.
Using both apps? On the final screen tap “Copy to the other account”, or use “Copy Stremio ↔ Nuvio” in step 1. The wizard backs up the account that receives the copy, and both apps end up using the same AIOStreams. Before applying, you can choose whether to keep addons only the other app had and, when copying to Nuvio, whether to add the home rows.
Several Nuvio profiles? When you finish, the wizard offers “Apply to the selected profiles” to copy the same setup to the account's other profiles, with a backup for each one.

Optional: track what you watch

Stremio and Nuvio already remember what you're watching in your account. If you also want a full history, watchlists or IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes ratings, you can connect a tracking service. It isn't needed to watch anything.

  • MDBList (recommended): create a free account at mdblist.com ↗, go to Preferences ↗, open “API Access” and copy your key. Paste it in step 4 of the wizard: AIOMetadata will record what you watch there. In Nuvio, paste the same key in Settings → Integrations → MDBList to see ratings on each title page. The free plan allows 1,000 requests a day, enough for personal use.
  • Simkl: free and good for history and watchlists. In Nuvio, connect it from Settings → Integrations.
  • Trakt: the best known, but in 2026 it limited free accounts (250 watchlist items and 5 lists) and restricted third-party apps. Use it only if you already have it: Stremio connects it in Settings → Trakt and Nuvio in Settings → Integrations.

You can use more than one at a time, but picking one is simplest.

FAQ

Can I use it without paying?

Yes. Choose “I don't have Debrid” in step 3 of the wizard. The experience may differ, though: videos may take longer to start, playback may stop, and some titles may have no links. Free sources vary in availability and some use P2P, which depends on other users sharing the files.

Is TorBox Essential required?

No. It's a suggestion to start with if you want Debrid. Choose another supported provider or continue without Debrid.

Do I need a new account if I already use Stremio or Nuvio?

No. Sign in with your current account. The backup is especially useful if you already have addons installed.

Is my app password the TorBox API key?

No. They're different things. The email and password connect your Stremio or Nuvio account. The API key connects TorBox to the wizard.

Is it safe to type my app password?

Your email and password go through the wizard's server only to sign in to Stremio or Nuvio, and they aren't stored. Backups stay encrypted in your own browser. The wizard's code is public: you can review it on GitHub.

What does Nuvio have that Stremio doesn't?

Nuvio lets you customize the home screen with collections: rows like Watch Now, Genres, Decades or International. The wizard adds them for you. If you use your own TMDB key in step 4, it also adds a Franchises row (Star Wars, Marvel and others). Nuvio also has separate profiles within the same account.

Can I run the wizard again?

Yes. Each time it creates a backup before changing anything, so you can try other options and go back.
